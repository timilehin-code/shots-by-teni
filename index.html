02About the lens

Not just
a video.

ShotsByTeni is a mobile videography brand built from a love of creativity, storytelling, and the quiet power of a well-held moment.

I create visually engaging films that preserve memories and help people share their ideas with feeling. My lens moves through events, personal brands, church and media projects, birthdays, weddings, and creative productions, looking for the details that make each story its own.

I'm open to collaborations, event coverage, brand projects, and creative opportunities that make room to learn, experiment, and grow.
